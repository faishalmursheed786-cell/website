About Me

Turning Complex Technology Into Systems That Simply Work.

I’m a Senior Network, Automation, AV, ELV & Infrastructure Engineer focused on designing, integrating, and commissioning high-performance technology systems for demanding residential and commercial environments.

My expertise spans enterprise networking, FortiGate security infrastructure, structured cabling, high-density IT infrastructure, luxury villa automation, ELV systems, and professional audio-visual solutions — bringing multiple technologies together into one reliable, intelligently engineered ecosystem.

I work across the complete project lifecycle — from interpreting architectural and MEP requirements and developing detailed AutoCAD shop drawings to hardware staging, rack infrastructure, network configuration, firewall provisioning, system integration, DSP programming, acoustic calibration, testing, commissioning, and final handover.

What sets my approach apart is the ability to look beyond individual systems:

  • A network is not just switches and cables.
  • An automation system is not just lighting and control.
  • An AV system is not just speakers and amplifiers.

Every system has to work together.

I focus on creating technology infrastructure that is stable, scalable, serviceable, and built for real-world performance — whether it is a high-end private residence, enterprise network, or integrated ELV and AV environment.

With 10+ home automation projects and 50+ network infrastructure projects delivered across Dubai, including Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills, Jumeirah Bay Island, and Downtown Dubai, I bring hands-on site experience together with engineering design and system-level thinking.
